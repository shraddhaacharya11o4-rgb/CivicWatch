About Electricity Department

The Electricity Department is responsible for managing and maintaining public electrical infrastructure, ensuring uninterrupted power supply, and monitoring electrical safety systems across urban and rural areas.

The department handles complaints related to streetlight failures, power interruptions, damaged electric poles, loose electrical wires, transformer issues, and public electrical hazards.

Through the CivicWatch platform, citizens can report electricity-related issues online by uploading complaint details, photographs, and exact locations for quick resolution and monitoring.

Key Responsibilities

Streetlight Maintenance

Monitoring and repairing damaged or non-functional streetlights to improve public safety.

Power Supply Monitoring

Ensuring continuous and stable electricity supply across residential and public areas.

Electrical Safety Inspection

Inspecting electrical poles, transformers, and wiring systems to prevent accidents and hazards.

Emergency Electrical Services

Providing emergency support during power failures, short circuits, and electrical breakdowns.

Services Provided to Citizens

The Electricity Department provides several important public services through the CivicWatch grievance management system:


  • Online reporting of streetlight failures
  • Monitoring public power supply interruptions
  • Electrical pole and transformer maintenance tracking
  • Complaint registration for damaged electrical wires
  • Public electrical safety inspections and issue reporting
  • Real-time grievance tracking and status updates
  • Emergency electrical maintenance notifications

Importance of the Department

Efficient electrical infrastructure is essential for public safety, economic development, transportation systems, and urban growth. The Electricity Department plays a major role in maintaining safe and reliable electrical services for citizens.

CivicWatch improves transparency and communication by allowing citizens to instantly report electrical issues and monitor government maintenance activities and complaint resolution progress in real-time.
