About Road Department

The Roads Department is one of the most important public infrastructure departments responsible for the construction, maintenance, repair, and monitoring of roads, highways, bridges, pavements, and traffic infrastructure across the city and rural areas.

The department ensures safe and smooth transportation for citizens by regularly monitoring road conditions and resolving public complaints related to potholes, damaged roads, drainage overflow, broken dividers, missing road signs, and incomplete construction activities.

Through the CivicWatch platform, citizens can directly report road-related grievances online by uploading complaint details, photographs, and exact locations. Government officers can inspect issues, update maintenance status, and monitor infrastructure progress in real-time.

Key Responsibilities

Pothole Repair

Immediate identification and repair of potholes and damaged roads to ensure public safety and smooth transportation.

Road Construction

Planning and monitoring new road construction projects, flyovers, and public transport infrastructure.

Traffic Safety

Installation of traffic signals, warning boards, speed breakers, and safety markings on public roads.

Infrastructure Inspection

Regular road inspections and condition monitoring to identify damages and maintenance requirements.

Services Provided to Citizens

The Roads Department provides several important public services through the CivicWatch grievance management system:


  • Online reporting of potholes and road damages
  • Monitoring ongoing road construction projects
  • Street and highway maintenance tracking
  • Road safety inspections and traffic infrastructure monitoring
  • Public complaint tracking and grievance resolution updates
  • Bridge and pavement condition monitoring
  • Emergency repair notifications and maintenance scheduling

Importance of the Department

Efficient road infrastructure is essential for economic development, emergency services, public transportation, and overall citizen safety. The Roads Department contributes significantly to improving transportation quality, reducing accidents, and maintaining better connectivity across urban and rural regions.

CivicWatch enhances transparency and accountability by allowing citizens to actively participate in reporting infrastructure problems and tracking government actions in real-time.
