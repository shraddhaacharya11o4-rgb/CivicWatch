About Sanitation Department

The Sanitation Department is responsible for maintaining cleanliness, waste management, public hygiene, and sanitation infrastructure across urban and rural regions.

The department handles complaints related to garbage collection, waste disposal, drainage cleaning, public area cleanliness, sewage overflow, and improper waste management activities.

Through the CivicWatch platform, citizens can report sanitation-related issues online by uploading complaint details, photographs, and exact locations for faster monitoring and resolution.

Key Responsibilities

Garbage Collection

Regular collection and disposal of household, commercial, and public waste materials.

Drainage Cleaning

Cleaning and maintenance of drainage systems to prevent waterlogging and sewage overflow.

Public Area Maintenance

Ensuring cleanliness in streets, parks, bus stops, and other public places.

Waste Management Monitoring

Monitoring waste disposal systems and sanitation infrastructure to improve environmental safety.

Services Provided to Citizens

The Sanitation Department provides several important public services through the CivicWatch grievance management system:


  • Online reporting of garbage collection issues
  • Monitoring drainage cleaning and sewage maintenance
  • Public cleanliness complaint management
  • Waste disposal and sanitation tracking
  • Real-time grievance tracking and complaint updates
  • Public hygiene monitoring and inspection services
  • Emergency sanitation maintenance notifications

Importance of the Department

Efficient sanitation management is essential for public health, environmental protection, and maintaining hygienic living conditions. The Sanitation Department contributes significantly to improving cleanliness, reducing pollution, and ensuring safe public spaces.

CivicWatch improves transparency and communication by enabling citizens to report sanitation problems instantly and monitor government cleaning and maintenance activities in real-time.
