About Water Supply Department

The Water Supply Department is responsible for managing water distribution, monitoring pipeline systems, maintaining water quality, and ensuring continuous water supply across urban and rural regions.

The department handles complaints related to water leakage, pipe damage, low water pressure, drainage overflow, contaminated water supply, and interrupted water services.

Through the CivicWatch platform, citizens can easily report water-related issues online by submitting complaints, uploading supporting photographs, and tracking grievance status in real-time.

Key Responsibilities

Water Leakage Repair

Detection and repair of damaged pipelines, leakage points, and water wastage issues.

Pipeline Maintenance

Regular monitoring and maintenance of underground water distribution pipelines and drainage systems.

Water Quality Monitoring

Ensuring safe and clean drinking water through regular quality inspections and testing.

Emergency Water Services

Providing emergency water supply support during shortages, repairs, and maintenance activities.

Services Provided to Citizens

The Water Supply Department provides important public services through the CivicWatch grievance management system:


  • Online reporting of water leakage complaints
  • Pipeline damage monitoring and maintenance
  • Drainage overflow complaint handling
  • Water supply interruption tracking
  • Water quality inspection and issue reporting
  • Public grievance status tracking and updates
  • Emergency maintenance scheduling and notifications

Importance of the Department

Efficient water management is essential for public health, sanitation, and sustainable urban development. The Water Supply Department ensures safe drinking water availability and proper maintenance of water infrastructure systems.

CivicWatch improves transparency and communication by enabling citizens to report water-related problems instantly and monitor government actions and maintenance progress in real-time.
